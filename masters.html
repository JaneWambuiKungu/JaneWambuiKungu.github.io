Seven questions to ask before you pay

Ask these of any university, including Unicaf. Get the answers in writing.

  1. Who awards the degree, and is it recognised where I want to work?Check with your education regulator and with employers in your target country.
  2. What scholarship percentage am I offered?Ask for the figure and the conditions for keeping it.
  3. What is the total cost, and what is the instalment schedule?Include any fees beyond tuition.
  4. How many hours a week does it take?Compare that with the hours you have, not the hours you hope for.
  5. How is it assessed?Coursework, exams, a dissertation, and the deadlines for each.
  6. What happens if I need to pause?Ask about deferral, and what happens to fees already paid.
  7. Does the curriculum match the jobs I want?Read the module list, then compare it with real job adverts.

Questions people ask

Will I get a discount through your link?

Unicaf's public pages do not list a discount for the person referred. They describe a scholarship and monthly instalments. Ask Unicaf what you qualify for, and get it in writing.

Why are you sharing this?

I completed my own master's and I think the right questions save people money. The link is a referral, and I may receive a reward if you enrol and pay. The checklist works for any university.

Is a master's worth it for me?

It depends on your goal and the hours you have. The quiz above gives you a starting point, and the checklist tells you what to verify.

Will employers accept the degree?

Check with your education regulator and with two or three employers you would like to work for before you pay.

Where do I ask Unicaf my questions?

Unicaf lists studentapply@unicaf.org for admissions and referafriend@unicaf.org for the referral programme.
